CoinMonitor Privacy Policy

Developer: baiyanwu · Updated: 12 October 2026

CoinMonitor is an open-source Android app. It has no developer-operated data server, requires no CoinMonitor account, and includes no advertising or analytics SDK. The app does not automatically upload diagnostic logs or sell user data.

Local data

Watchlists, saved addresses, settings, history and caches are stored on your device. Wallet recovery phrases, private keys and API credentials are encrypted locally. Wallet signing happens on the device; the app does not automatically send recovery phrases or private keys to external services. API authentication is sent to the relevant provider when required.

Third-party services

Market and wallet features directly contact providers such as Binance, OKX, GMGN, Pump.fun, DexScreener, GeckoTerminal, CoinGecko, Alchemy and configured blockchain RPC services. Requests may include search terms, token contracts, public wallet addresses and transaction information. Recipients can also receive your IP address and request metadata. Floating monitoring can continue these requests in the background. Confirmed signed transactions are broadcast to the blockchain.

Clipboard lookup reads text when you open its panel and uses recognized addresses or links for queries; FoMo profile handles are sent to the third-party service 985monitor.xyz. DApps can use cookies and receive browser data, plus public wallet addresses or signatures after your approval. Optional AI actions send your question and included token or market context to your chosen AI service. Third-party processing and retention follow each provider's policy.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until removed through app controls, clearing Android app storage or uninstalling. Android backup may include some app data; the wallet vault is excluded. Manage backup copies through system settings. Local deletion cannot remove public blockchain records or data already received by third parties.

Contact

For privacy questions, use CoinMonitor GitHub Issues. Issues are public; do not post wallet secrets or API keys. This policy and its update date will change when data handling changes.

CoinMonitor 隐私政策

开发者：baiyanwu · 更新日期：2026 年 10 月 12 日

CoinMonitor 是开源 Android 应用，没有开发者自建的数据服务器，无需注册 CoinMonitor 账号，未集成广告或统计分析 SDK，不自动上传诊断日志，也不出售用户数据。

本地数据

自选列表、保存的地址、设置、历史和缓存保存在设备上。钱包助记词、私钥及 API 凭证在本地加密保存。钱包签名在设备上完成，应用不会自动向外部服务发送助记词或私钥；请求所需的 API 认证信息会发送给对应服务。

第三方服务

行情和钱包功能直接请求 Binance、OKX、GMGN、Pump.fun、DexScreener、GeckoTerminal、CoinGecko、Alchemy 及所配置的区块链 RPC 等第三方服务。请求可能包含搜索词、代币合约、公开钱包地址和交易信息，接收方也可能获得 IP 地址及请求信息。悬浮盯盘可以在后台继续请求；确认后的已签名交易会广播到区块链。

剪贴板查询在打开面板时读取文本，用识别出的地址或链接发起查询；FoMo 主页标识发送给第三方 985monitor.xyz。DApp 可使用 Cookie 并获取浏览器信息，经您批准后还可获取公开钱包地址或签名。可选 AI 功能把您的问题及附带的代币或行情信息发送给所选 AI 服务。第三方如何处理和保留数据，按各自政策执行。

保留与删除

本地数据保留至您通过应用功能删除、清除 Android 应用存储或卸载应用。部分应用数据可能参与 Android 系统备份，钱包保险库已排除；备份副本请通过系统设置管理。本地删除不会移除区块链公开记录或第三方已经接收的数据。

联系

隐私问题请通过 CoinMonitor GitHub Issues 联系。Issue 内容公开，请勿提交钱包秘密信息或 API 密钥。数据处理方式变化时，会更新本政策及日期。
