CoinMonitor Privacy Policy

CoinMonitor is an open-source app maintained by baiyanwu. It requires no account and has no developer-operated user-data server.

1. Local storage

Watchlists, settings and history stay on your device. Wallet recovery phrases, private keys and API credentials are encrypted locally. Wallets sign locally; the app does not automatically upload recovery phrases or private keys.

2. Third-party services

External market, address lookup, RPC, AI and DApp services may receive public addresses, profile handles, queries, authentication and your IP, under their own retention policies. Clipboard text is read when you open its panel. AI services receive your prompts; DApps may use cookies. Approved signatures or transactions go to the relevant service.

3. No tracking or sale

The app includes no advertising or behavioral analytics SDK, does not sell user data, and does not automatically upload diagnostic logs.

4. Retention and deletion

Local data stays until you delete it; clear app storage or uninstall to remove it. System backup may include some data, excluding the wallet vault. Manage backups through system settings. Local deletion does not erase blockchain records or third-party data.

5. Contact

For privacy questions, use GitHub Issues. Issues are public; do not post private keys or credentials. This page is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses.
